    How to play

    1. 1
      Find the gray patch. Each day is a public-domain painting with one part's color taken out. Its shading is still there; its color isn't.
    2. 2
      Pick a color. Drag the ring for the hue and the square for how vivid (left–right) and how bright (up–down). The patch repaints as you go, so judge it against the rest of the painting.
    3. 3
      Test it. You get a match % and which way to go: lighter or darker, redder or greener, yellower or bluer. Then adjust and test again.
    4. 4
      Lock in. Your score is your best match, minus 3 for each test after the first. Up to 6 tests.

    A new painting every day at midnight, from the Art Institute of Chicago's open-access collection. The answer is the average color of the patch in the original. Color names come from the xkcd Color Survey (public domain).
