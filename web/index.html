      How to play

      1. 1
        Read the clue. An AI looked at today's secret color and described it in words, without naming it.
      2. 2
        Pick a color. Drag around the ring to choose the hue, then in the square to set how vivid (left–right) and how bright (up–down) it is. Arrow keys work too.
      3. 3
        Test it. You get a match % and which way to go: lighter or darker, redder or greener, yellower or bluer. Then move the picker and test again.
      4. 4
        Lock in. Your score is your best match, minus 3 for each test after the first and 5 for each extra clue. Up to 6 tests.

      A new color every day at midnight. Clues are written by an AI (an LLM) and only kept if a second AI, reading them cold, can get close to the color. Colors come from the xkcd Color Survey (public domain).
